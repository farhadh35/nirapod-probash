Privacy Policy

Nirapod Probash (নিরাপদ প্রবাস) · Last updated 27 August 2026

In one sentence

Nirapod Probash has no account, collects nothing about you, and uploads nothing. It uses the internet for exactly one thing — downloading updates to the public register of recruiting agencies — and that is a one-way download. Your documents, your searches and your calculations never leave your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no registration, no login, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting and no unique identifier. We do not know who you are, that you installed the app, or what you looked at in it.

Your documents

The document vault (কাগজপত্র) lets you photograph your passport, visa or contract so you keep a copy if an agency takes the originals. Those images:

  • are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, using a key generated on your phone and held in the Android keystore;
  • are stored only in the app's private storage — they are never written to your photo gallery, so they do not appear in a cloud photo backup;
  • are never uploaded — the app has no upload path at all, and no screen that transmits anything you type or photograph;
  • are protected behind your phone's own lock (PIN, pattern or fingerprint), because phones in this community are often shared.
Deleting a document overwrites the file and removes it. Uninstalling the app deletes all of them. Neither we nor anyone else can recover them — keep paper copies too.

Permissions, and why each one exists

INTERNET
Only to download updated agency-register and cost data from our server. The app asks for data; it never sends any. The request carries no body, no identifier and no account, so the server cannot tell one phone from another.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
So a failed update can honestly say “no connection” rather than blaming the server.
Camera / photo access
Only when you tap “যোগ করুন” to add a document. Used for that image and nothing else. The app holds no camera or storage permission of its own — it uses the system camera and the Android photo picker.
USE_BIOMETRIC
USE_FINGERPRINT
To unlock the vault. The check runs entirely on your phone; we never receive your fingerprint or PIN.

Agency data in the app

The recruiting-agency licence records shipped with the app are a copy of the public register published by the Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training (BMET/RAIMS). They are public government data about businesses, not about you. Searching happens on your phone; your searches are not sent anywhere.

Links out of the app

Some advice cards offer a button to open an official website — for example bangladeshcustoms.gov.bd. Tapping it hands the address to your browser and you leave the app. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing to work abroad. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version ever sends anything from your phone to us, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here in plain words rather than buried.

Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@nirapodprobash.bd
